LXGW WenKai is an open-source Chinese typeface derived from Fontworks' Klee One. Its blend of regular-script and Song-style forms gives text a handwritten, literary character suited to poetry, annotations, and medium-length reading.

The family expands Klee One with extensive Simplified and Traditional Chinese coverage while also supporting Japanese, Korean, Unicode variation sequences, and vertical typesetting. It is available in Light, Regular, and Medium weights.
