Lextrall is a variable sans-serif based on Lexend, the typeface developed by Bonnie Shaver-Troup and Thomas Jockin to support reading fluency. Luka “Mahtaran” Leer expanded that foundation with small capitals and old-style figures, giving it a richer typographic vocabulary.

Its design space spans weight from Thin to Black, while a custom expansion axis allows the proportions of the letterforms to be adjusted.
