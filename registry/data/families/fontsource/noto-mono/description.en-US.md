Noto Mono is an archived monospaced member of the Noto family with a smaller repertoire than the main Noto Sans and Serif families. It was originally introduced from Droid Sans Mono under the Noto name and later published in a complete nine-weight family.

This source preserves the final distinct Noto Mono release. It is not substituted with the separately named and currently maintained Noto Sans Mono family.
