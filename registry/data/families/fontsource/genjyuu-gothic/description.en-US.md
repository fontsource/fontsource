Gen Jyuu Gothic is a rounded Japanese sans-serif derived from the Japanese portion of Source Han Sans, with additional glyphs from M+ Outline Fonts. Its softened corners give the family the character of a rounded Gothic while preserving the broad text coverage and vertical-writing support of its source.

This package contains the standard Gen Jyuu Gothic family in seven weights. The upstream release also provides separately named proportional, monospaced, and alternate-rounding families, which are not folded into this identity.
