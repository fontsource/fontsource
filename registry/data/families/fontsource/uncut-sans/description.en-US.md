Uncut Sans is a slightly quirky sans-serif by Kasper Nordkvist, originally designed for UNCUT.wtf. It avoids a particular historical model or deeper design manifesto, leaving it with an intentionally hard-to-place personality.

The variable family covers a broad range of weights and upright-to-italic styles. Its OpenType stylistic sets provide alternate letters, figures, punctuation marks, rounded punctuation, and arrows.
