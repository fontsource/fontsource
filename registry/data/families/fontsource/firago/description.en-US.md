FiraGO is a multilingual extension of Fira Sans designed as a separate family so both can be installed without conflict. It retains Fira Sans 4.3's Latin, IPA, Pan-African, Cyrillic, and polytonic Greek coverage while adding Arabic, Devanagari, Georgian, Hebrew, and Thai.

The family was developed by Carrois Corporate and bBox Type with specialist designers and consultants for each added script. Its TrueType builds include manual basic hinting and nine standard CSS weights in upright and italic styles.
