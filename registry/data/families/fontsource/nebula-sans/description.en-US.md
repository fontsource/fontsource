Nebula Sans is a versatile humanist sans-serif with a neutral tone and a focus on legibility in digital interfaces and print. Developed as Nebula's brand typeface, it is based on Source Sans 3 and adapts its defaults and alternates for continuity with the service's former Whitney SSm typography.

The family includes six weights from Light to Black in upright and italic styles, with tabular figures and stylistic alternates for the a, g, and l.
